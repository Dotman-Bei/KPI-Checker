Check your Botchain project

Paste your mainnet contract and your website. We read the contract's activity on BotScan, open your site, check its footer for the two required links, and tell you exactly what to fix.

BOT Chain mainnet only (chain ID 677). Testnet contracts are not accepted.

The page whose footer should link to Botchain and BotScan.

Reading BotScan and opening your website. This usually takes 10–20 seconds.

A project passes when its mainnet contract shows real use and its website footer points back to BOT Chain.

Five wallets. At least five different wallets must have sent a transaction to the contract. We count the senders of the contract's transactions on BotScan mainnet.

Ten transactions. The contract needs at least ten transactions in total on BOT Chain mainnet, the same number BotScan shows on the contract's page.

Botchain link. The website footer must contain a clickable link to https://botchain.ai/. Plain text, a link elsewhere on the page, or a link to a subpage does not count.

BotScan link. The footer must also link to https://scan.botchain.ai/, the mainnet explorer. A testnet explorer link or a link to one address page does not count.
